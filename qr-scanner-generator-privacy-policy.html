Privacy Policy

QR Scanner & Generator
Last Updated: July 7, 2026

1. Introduction

QR Scanner & Generator ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe experience while using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

The app is developed and maintained by Hello Bangladeshi, a technology initiative focused on creating innovative digital solutions for safety and community well-being.

By using QR Scanner & Generator, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Device Information: Unique device identifiers, Android ID, and device model (for cloud sync and personalized features)
  • User Preferences: App configurations and settings (stored locally)
  • QR Code Data: Text, URLs, email addresses, phone numbers, contact details, or Wi-Fi credentials you generate (processed locally only)
  • In-App Purchases: Premium subscription status (stored securely via Google Play Billing)

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, and time spent (to improve user experience)
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash data via Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability
  • Performance Data: App performance metrics to optimize user experience

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Play Services: For location, maps, push notifications, and billing
  • Firebase: For analytics and crash reporting
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Purposes

  • QR Scanning & Generation: Provide QR code and barcode scanning and generation functionality
  • Personalize Experience: Customize app settings based on your preferences
  • Improve Services: Analyze usage patterns to enhance app functionality and user experience

3.2 Secondary Purposes

  • Premium Features Management: Track subscriptions and unlocked features (via Firebase and Google Play)
  • Display Advertisements: Show ads via Google AdMob
  • Analytics: Understand user behavior to make data-driven improvements
  • Communication: Send important notifications about app updates and important information

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where We Store Data

  • Local Storage: Preferences, settings, and generated QR codes are stored locally on your device (encrypted using Android's built-in security)
  • Firebase Cloud: Premium features and analytics data are stored securely in Firebase (encrypted at rest and in transit)
  • Google Cloud: Analytics and crash data are stored on Google's secure servers (anonymized)

4.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to user data (strictly controlled)
  • Regular Audits: We regularly review our security practices to protect your information
  • Data Minimization: We only collect data that is necessary for app functionality

4.3 Data Retention

  • Local Data: Remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Cloud Data: Retained as long as you use the app or until you request deletion
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized after 12 months

5. Permissions We Request

5.1 Required Permissions

  • Camera: Used to scan QR codes and barcodes
  • Storage: Used to save generated QR codes locally on your device
  • Internet: To sync with Firebase and AdMob
  • Vibrate: To provide haptic feedback when scanning is successful

5.2 Optional Permissions

  • Camera Flash: To use flash while scanning in dark environments
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Required to show notification alerts
Important Note: We never access your camera or storage without your permission. All QR scanning and generation is processed locally and is never uploaded to our servers.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Privacy Controls in the App

  • Camera Access: Enable/disable camera access anytime in your device settings
  • Scan History: Clear scan history anytime within the app
  • Sound & Vibration: Control alert sounds and vibration in settings

6.2 Data Access and Deletion

  • View Your Data: You can view your preferences and settings in the app
  • Clear Local Data: Clear all locally stored data via device settings (Settings → Apps → QR Scanner & Generator → Clear Data)
  • Request Cloud Data Deletion: Contact us at support.hellobangladeshi@gmail.com with your Device ID (found in app settings). We will process your request within 30 days.

6.3 Your Rights Under GDPR / CCPA

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to Data Portability: Request data in a structured format
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of data collection (we do not sell data)

7. Children's Privacy

QR Scanner & Generator is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.
