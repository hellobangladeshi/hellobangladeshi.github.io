About Us

Building bridges across continents, preserving heritage, and fostering growth

Our Mission

Hello Bangladeshi Ltd is a pioneering digital platform dedicated to uniting Bangladeshis worldwide. We believe in the power of community, culture, and connection to create meaningful experiences that transcend geographical boundaries.

Through innovative technology and cultural appreciation, we provide a comprehensive ecosystem where Bangladeshis can celebrate their heritage, connect with their roots, and build lasting relationships with fellow community members around the globe.

Our platform serves as a cultural hub, offering engaging content, educational resources, entertainment, and networking opportunities designed specifically for the global Bangladeshi diaspora.

Meet Our Founder

Farabi Walid

Founder & CEO, Hello Bangladeshi Ltd

Farabi Walid is a visionary entrepreneur and community builder dedicated to connecting Bangladeshis worldwide. With a passion for technology, culture, and community development, Farabi founded Hello Bangladeshi Ltd to create a platform that celebrates Bangladeshi heritage while fostering global connections.

Through innovative digital solutions and a deep understanding of community needs, Farabi continues to lead Hello Bangladeshi Ltd in its mission to unite, educate, and empower the global Bangladeshi diaspora.
