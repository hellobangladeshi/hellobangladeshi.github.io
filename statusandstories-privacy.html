Privacy Policy for Status & Stories

Last Updated: March 2025

Welcome to Status & Stories ("the App"), developed and operated by Hello Bangladeshi (Muhammad Walid Ahmed). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, transparent mobile experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect and Use

A. Local App Data (On-Device Storage)

The App operates primarily offline and stores content locally on your device using Room Database and internal storage:

  • Favorites & Saved Items: Statuses, quotes, and short stories you mark as favorite are saved locally on your phone.
  • Quote Maker Studio Creations: Images generated using our Quote Maker Studio are saved directly to your device's storage. We do not upload or store your personal images on external servers.

B. Automatically Collected Data

While using our App, certain non-personal technical information may be collected automatically by third-party SDKs integrated into the App to provide advertising, analytics, and app functionality:

  • Device information (Device Model, OS Version, Language Settings)
  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID for personalized or non-personalized ads)
  • App performance data (Crash logs, performance metrics, and usage statistics)

2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App utilizes trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their respective privacy policies. Direct links to third-party privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob & UMP (Consent SDK)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Remote Config
  • Google Play In-App Billing

3. Advertising & User Consent (AdMob & GDPR)

Our App serves advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To comply with privacy laws worldwide (including GDPR and Google's EU User Consent Policy):

  • We integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to ask for your explicit consent regarding personalized ads where legally required.
  • You can manage or reset your advertising preferences at any time from your device's system settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate steps to remove such information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your user data (like favorite statuses, downloads, and custom images) is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • You can clear your favorites or download history at any time from the App Settings screen.
  • Uninstalling the App will automatically erase all local data saved by the App.

6. Data Security

We value your trust in using our application. We implement industry-standard security measures provided by the Android OS framework and official Google Jetpack libraries to protect your local data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
