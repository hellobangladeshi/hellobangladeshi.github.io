Privacy Policy

Effective: August 21, 2026

QuizzerX Pro is a premium quiz app built with privacy at its core. Developed by Farabi Walid / Hello Bangladeshi.

1. Information Collection

QuizzerX Pro is an offline-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No PII – We never ask for your name, email, phone, or location.
  • No accounts – No sign‑up, no login, no profile.
  • Local only – Your scores, streaks, and achievements stay on your device using Room database. They never leave your phone.

2. Third‑Party Services

The App uses Google Play Services for distribution and basic analytics. Their privacy policy applies:

  • Google Play Services – for updates and platform metrics.

3. Log Data & Crash Reports

If the App crashes, we may receive anonymized diagnostic data via Google Play Console. This includes:

  • Device model & OS version (to help us fix bugs).
  • No personally identifiable information is ever attached.

4. Advertising & Cookies

Zero ads, zero trackers. QuizzerX Pro is a premium, ad‑free experience. We do not use cookies, web beacons, or any tracking mechanism.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has shared data, please reach out to us.

6. Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device’s built‑in protections. We recommend keeping your system and apps updated.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
