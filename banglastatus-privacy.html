Privacy Policy for Bangla Status

Effective Date: October 2024 | Last Updated: March 2025

Summary: Welcome to Bangla Status (বাংলা স্ট্যাটাস), operated by HelloBangladeshi. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles data, permissions, third-party services, and user privacy rights in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Overview & Core Features

Bangla Status is a lifestyle and quotes application providing curated Bengali statuses, quotes, Islamic content, special days greetings, and a Quote Maker studio. The app is designed to work seamlessly offline and online. Most created graphics, saved quotes, and favorites are stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal identifiers like name, phone number, or address. However, certain non-personal and device technical data is collected automatically through third-party services integrated into the app:

  • Device Identifiers & Technical Information: IP address, device model, operating system version, system language, and unique Advertising ID (AAID).
  • App Usage & Analytics Data: App launches, screen views, feature usage, and performance statistics.
  • User Generated Content (Local Storage): Quotes created in Quote Maker, background images selected from device gallery, and favorite lists are stored strictly on your local device. We do not upload your personal photos or custom quotes to any external server.
  • In-App Purchases / Billing Data: Premium subscriptions or one-time purchases ("Bangla Status Pro") are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not store or process your credit card, debit card, or banking details.

3. Permissions Used in the App

Our app strictly requests only essential system permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch online quote updates, Remote Config settings, display advertisements, and process Google Play Billing requests.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Max API 28): Used only on legacy Android versions (Android 9 and lower) to save generated quote images to your device's photo gallery.
  • System Photo Picker: Used when choosing custom background photos in Quote Maker. It operates via Android's secure system picker and requires no broad photo library permissions.

To support app maintenance, monetization, and feature improvements, we use trusted Google SDKs. These third-party providers may collect anonymous data subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android functionality and security.

    AdMob & Privacy Info

  • Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config & Firestore): Analyzes aggregated usage data and delivers dynamic app settings.
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles secure payment processing for Bangla Status Pro.

5. GDPR / European Economic Area (EEA) Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK), you have specific privacy rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our app utilizes the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to provide you with explicit control over ad personalization and cookies consent upon opening the app. You may modify your consent choices at any time through the app settings or device privacy controls.

6. Children's Privacy

Bangla Status is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through our app, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt action to remove such information.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All user favorites, downloaded quotes, and created images are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the application or clearing app data via Android Settings will permanently remove all locally saved data. Anonymous analytics and advertising data collected by Google services are retained according to Google's standard retention schedules.

8. Security

We prioritize data security. All network transmissions with Firebase and Google services use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically for changes.
