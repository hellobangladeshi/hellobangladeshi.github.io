Privacy Policy

The Monkey Jump — Your privacy is our priority

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

1. Introduction

The Monkey Jump ("we", "our", or "us") is a fun and exciting platformer game app. This app is an initiative of Hello Bangladeshi.

Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. By using this app, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. Information We Collect

📱 Automatically Collected Information:

  • Device Information — Model, OS version, unique device ID.
  • Gameplay Data — Score, levels, completed challenges, time played, correct/incorrect answers.
  • App Usage Data — Which features are used, session duration, crash reports.
  • IP Address — For approximate location and security purposes.

📊 Third-Party Services:

  • Google AdMob — For showing personalized ads based on device ID and usage.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — For analyzing app performance and usage.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — For collecting and analyzing app crash data.
  • Google Play Services — For in-app purchases and authentication.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • ✅ To improve app performance and user experience.
  • ✅ To track game scores and progress.
  • ✅ To show relevant advertisements (AdMob).
  • ✅ To identify and fix bugs and crashes.
  • ✅ To analyze how the app is used and make improvements.

4. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal information to third parties. However, data may be shared in the following cases:

  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or valid court order.
  • Service Providers: Firebase, AdMob, and Google Analytics have their own privacy policies.
  • Public Display: Leaderboard shows your username and score to other players.

Third-Party Services Used:

  • • Google Firebase — Database, authentication, crash reporting
  • • Google AdMob — Banner and interstitial ads
  • • Google Analytics for Firebase — Usage analytics
  • • Google Play Billing — In-app purchases

5. Data Security

  • 🔐 Encryption: Sensitive data is stored in encrypted format.
  • 🔐 Firebase Security Rules: Strict access control.
  • 🔐 Regular Audits: We regularly review security measures.

⚠️ Please note that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We strive to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. In-App Purchases

Our app offers In-App Purchases (Pro version, Ad-Free package, Extra Lives, etc.). All financial transactions are handled securely by Google Play Billing. We do not store your credit card or financial information on our servers.

8. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

We use AdMob to display ads to keep the app free. AdMob may use your device data to show personalized ads. If you prefer not to receive personalized ads, you can opt out via your device's Google Ad Settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

9. App Permissions

  • Internet Access: Required for loading ads, analytics, and syncing game data.
  • Network State: To check internet connection status.

10. Data Deletion Request

As we do not collect personal accounts or store user data on our servers, users can delete all local data (coins, levels, progress) by simply uninstalling the application from their device. If you have any concerns or wish to request the removal of any analytics data associated with your device ID, please contact us at support.hellobangladeshi@gmail.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page. We will notify you of any significant changes via the app or email.

13. Local Storage & User-Provided Name

  • No Personal Data Collected on Servers: The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as your name or email address) to our servers.
  • Username Stored Locally: Any name or username you choose to set within the app is stored only on your device using Local Storage. This information is used solely for in-app personalization and leaderboard display.
  • Data Deletion on Uninstall: If you uninstall the app or clear the app's data from your device settings, all locally stored information (including your username and game progress) will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
  • No Sharing or Selling: We never share, sell, or transfer your locally stored username or any other personal data to third parties.

14. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The Monkey Jump is designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • ✅ No personal information is required to play the game.
  • ✅ All game data is stored locally on your device.
  • ✅ We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • ✅ You are in complete control of your data — you can delete it anytime by clearing the app data or uninstalling.

We believe that you should enjoy the game without worrying about your privacy. That is why we keep data collection to an absolute minimum and never misuse the information we have access to.
