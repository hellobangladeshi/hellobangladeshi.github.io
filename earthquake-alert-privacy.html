Privacy Policy

Earthquake Alert Pro
Last Updated: June 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Earthquake Alert Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe experience while using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Earthquake Alert Pro is developed and maintained by Hello Bangladeshi (hellobangladeshi.github.io), a technology initiative focused on creating innovative digital solutions. This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Earthquake Alert Pro mobile application and not to any other products or services of Hello Bangladeshi.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Device Information: Unique device identifiers, Android ID, and device model
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate location information when you grant permission
  • User Preferences: Your alert settings, filter preferences, and app configurations
  • Family Member Information: Names and phone numbers you voluntarily add for safety alerts

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, and time spent
  • Earthquake Data: Information about earthquakes you view or interact with
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous crash data to improve app stability (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Performance Data: App performance metrics to optimize user experience

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • USGS Earthquake Data: Real-time earthquake information from the U.S. Geological Survey
  • Google Play Services: For location services, maps, and push notifications

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Purposes

  • Deliver Earthquake Alerts: Send you timely notifications about earthquakes near your location
  • Personalize Experience: Customize alerts based on your magnitude and distance preferences
  • Family Safety Features: Send "I am Safe" messages to your selected family members
  • Improve Services: Analyze usage patterns to enhance app functionality and user experience

3.2 Secondary Purposes

  • Premium Features Management: Track your premium subscriptions and unlocked features
  • Coin System: Manage your in-app coins for unlocking premium features
  • Analytics: Understand user behavior to make data-driven improvements
  • Communication: Send important notifications about app updates and safety information

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where We Store Data

  • Local Storage: Your preferences, settings, and cached earthquake data are stored locally on your device
  • Firebase Cloud: Premium features, coin balances, and family member information are stored securely in Firebase Firestore
  • Google Cloud: Analytics and crash data are stored on Google's secure servers

4.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to user data
  • Secure Authentication: Device-based authentication ensures only you can access your data
  • Regular Audits: We regularly review our security practices to protect your information

4.3 Data Retention

  • Local Data: Remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Cloud Data: Retained as long as you use the app or until you request deletion
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized after 12 months

5. Permissions We Request

5.1 Required Permissions

  • Location (Fine/Coarse): To show earthquakes near your location and provide accurate distance information
  • Notifications: To send you earthquake alerts and safety notifications
  • Internet: To fetch earthquake data from USGS servers and sync with Firebase

5.2 Optional Permissions

  • Background Location: To monitor for earthquakes when the app is not actively in use (can be disabled)
  • SMS: To send "I am Safe" messages to family members (only with your explicit permission)
  • Vibration: To provide haptic feedback during earthquake alerts
  • Flashlight: To use flashlight as a visual alert during emergencies
Important Note on SMS Permission: We only request SMS permission exclusively for the "I am Safe" feature. Messages are only sent to family members you have pre-approved in the app. We do not read, store, access your SMS inbox, or send any promotional messages. This feature is entirely optional and requires your explicit consent.
Important Note on Background Location: This permission is only used to detect earthquakes even when the app is in the background. You can disable this in your device settings at any time without losing core functionality.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Privacy Controls

  • Location Settings: You can enable/disable location access anytime in your device settings
  • Notification Preferences: Customize which alerts you receive in the app settings
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all locally stored data by clearing app data in device settings
  • Family Members: You have full control to add, edit, or delete family member information

6.2 Data Access and Deletion

  • View Your Data: You can view your preferences and settings in the app
  • Export Data: Premium users can export earthquake history as PDF
  • Request Cloud Data Deletion: To request deletion of your cloud-stored data, please contact us at info.hellobangladeshi@gmail.com with your Device ID (found in the app settings). We will process your request within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Earthquake Alert Pro is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.

8. Third-Party Services

8.1 Services We Use

  • Google Play Services: For location, maps, and billing
  • Firebase: For cloud storage, analytics, and crash reporting
  • USGS API: For earthquake data
  • AdMob: For displaying advertisements (in free version)
  • Google Billing: For processing premium subscriptions

8.2 Third-Party Privacy Policies

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • USGS Privacy Policy

9. Data Sharing

9.1 When We Share Data

  • With Your Consent: We share data when you explicitly grant permission (e.g., SMS)
  • For Safety: To send emergency messages to your designated family members
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Service Providers: With trusted third parties who assist in app operations

9.2 What We Never Share

  • We never sell your personal information
  • We never share your location data with advertisers
  • We never share your family member information without your explicit consent
AdMob Data Sharing: We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect anonymized data for ad personalization using your device's advertising ID. You can opt-out of ad personalization in your Google account settings or reset your advertising ID anytime. We do not share your location or personal data with AdMob.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers in compliance with applicable laws, including standard contractual clauses where required.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • Sending a notification (if you have enabled notifications)

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

  • Right to Rectification: You can correct inaccurate data

14. Specific Features Privacy

14.1 Earthquake Alerts

  • Alerts are based on your location and preferences
  • No permanent recording of your location history
  • Distance calculations are performed locally on your device

14.2 Family Safety Features

  • Phone numbers are stored securely and encrypted
  • SMS messages are sent only with your explicit permission
  • You control which members receive safety messages

14.3 Shake Detection (Offline Mode)

  • Uses device sensors only
  • No data is transmitted externally

  • No external transmission of your preparedness status
